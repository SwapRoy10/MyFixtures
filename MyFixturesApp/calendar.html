    How to subscribe

    Google Calendar: on the desktop website, open Settings → Add calendar → From URL and paste the link above. Apple Calendar: choose New Calendar Subscription. Outlook: choose Add calendar → Subscribe from web.

    We refresh team schedules about hourly. Your calendar app controls when it checks the subscription, so updates may take several hours. Unconfirmed times appear below but are excluded from the feed until confirmed. Previously exported entries are withdrawn if their time becomes unconfirmed.

    If you previously imported these games, remove that old import to avoid duplicates. This subscription cannot edit events from an earlier import.

    Manage or share this calendar
